# GRAPHOS-ACCEPTANCE-001 — evidence acceptance freeze

**Owner:** graph-os for its own requirement acceptance gate. **Status:** SPECIFIED; acceptance NOT_AUDITED. See [requirements.md](requirements.md) for the definition of every requirement ID and [status.json](status.json) for its delivery state and evidence. This spec does not change `scripts/check_public_specs.py`'s evidence schema; it freezes the exact subset of that schema a GraphOS requirement must carry before a human auditor may move its `acceptance_state` from `NOT_AUDITED` to `ACCEPTED`.

## User outcome

A reviewer opening any GraphOS spec's `status.json` can tell, without reading implementation source, whether a `LANDED` requirement is merely merged or is actually `ACCEPTED`: proven live on the default branch, exercised through a real entry point, and currently operational. A requirement cannot self-promote from `LANDED` to `ACCEPTED` by accumulating more delivery-lane commits; only a named auditor, acting on a checker's report, may record the promotion, and the recorded evidence may not later be edited to manufacture a pass.

## Requirements

| ID | Observable requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| FR-1 | Define the exact evidence-kind set a `LANDED` requirement must carry to earn `ACCEPTED`: a `merged_head` receipt naming a commit on the default branch, a `test` receipt at that same commit naming a test identifier reached through a real entry point (not a unit mock), and an operational receipt (`consumer` or `release`) at that same commit proving the row is live. | A requirement missing any one of the three evidence kinds, or with evidence recorded at a different commit than its `merged_head`, is reported as a gap and cannot be marked `ACCEPTED`. |
| FR-2 | Ship a typed acceptance-evidence model and a checker that, given a spec's `status.json`, lists every `LANDED` (or `CLOSED`) requirement lacking one or more of the FR-1 evidence kinds at its `merged_head` commit, naming the missing kind(s) per requirement. | Running the checker against a fixture `status.json` with a fully evidenced row and a gapped row reports exactly the gapped row's ID and missing kind(s); a malformed or non-existent `status.json` refuses rather than reporting a false pass. |
| FR-3 | Restrict who may change a requirement's `acceptance_state` to `ACCEPTED`: only a reviewer distinct from the delivery lane that produced the `merged_head` commit, acting after the FR-2 checker reports no gap for that requirement, may record the change. The implementing agent may propose evidence but may not self-attest acceptance. | The audit trail (PR review, commit author of the `status.json` edit, or an explicit auditor field) shows a reviewer identity different from the implementation commit's author for every `ACCEPTED` transition. |
| FR-4 | Make acceptance evidence append-only once recorded: an existing evidence entry's `kind`, `commit`, `result`, or `url` is never edited in place to flip a prior failure to a pass. A correction adds a new entry; the superseded entry is retained. | A diff that rewrites an existing evidence entry's `result`, `commit`, or `url` field instead of appending a new entry is rejected by review; the checker in FR-2 only trusts entries matching the current `merged_head` commit. |
| FR-5 | Operational evidence expires: a `consumer` or `release` receipt used to satisfy FR-1 carries an observation point, and a stale receipt (recorded against a commit no longer at the branch tip, or superseded by a later `merged_head`) no longer counts toward `ACCEPTED` for the current commit. | The FR-2 checker treats operational evidence recorded at a commit other than the requirement's current `merged_head` as missing, not as satisfying the gap. |

This spec owns the GraphOS acceptance-audit gate, not the evidence schema itself (`scripts/check_public_specs.py` remains authoritative for `EVIDENCE_KINDS`, `DELIVERY_STATES`, and `ACCEPTANCE_STATES`) and not deployment authorization. No row becomes `ACCEPTED` here from a local claim; acceptance requires the exact merged commit and the three evidence kinds in FR-1 recorded against it.
